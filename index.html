Clip Review is a personal, non-commercial desktop tool. Its owner uses it to edit short gaming clips on their own computer, review them, and place the ones they approve into the drafts of their own TikTok account, or publish them to their own Instagram account.

The tool never publishes anything the owner hasn't approved. TikTok videos arrive as drafts, and the owner decides in the TikTok app whether to post them. Instagram Reels are published only after the owner approves each one.

How it uses TikTok

  • Login Kit (user.info.basic): to confirm which TikTok account is connected.
  • Content Posting API (video.upload): to upload a finished video to that account's drafts.

How it uses Instagram

  • Instagram API with Instagram Login (instagram_business_basic, instagram_business_content_publish): to publish a clip as a Reel on the owner's own Instagram account, only after the owner has approved that clip.

The tool is used only by its owner, with their own account. It is not offered to the public.
