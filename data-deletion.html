Data deletion

AboutTerms of ServicePrivacy PolicyData deletion

Effective 17 November 2026

Clip Review keeps no data on any server. Everything it stores (TikTok and Instagram access tokens, and the clips it makes) is on the owner's own computer.

To delete your data

  1. Instagram: in the Instagram app go to Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites, and remove Clip Review. Meta then stops Clip Review's access.
  2. TikTok: in TikTok go to Settings → Security → Manage app permissions, and remove Clip Review.
  3. Local copies: delete the token files in the tool's working folder (or delete the tool). Nothing else is kept.

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue on this site's GitHub repository.
